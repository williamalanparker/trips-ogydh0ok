Trains: Tokyo, Mt Fuji, Kyoto

  • Route: Tokyo to Mt Fuji on Apr 18. Mt Fuji to Kyoto on Apr 20, usually via Mishima on the shinkansen. Kyoto to Tokyo on Apr 24 for the flight.
  • Big suitcases: Bags with total dimensions 161 to 250 cm need a seat with an oversized baggage area, reserved when you buy the ticket.
  • smartEX: Opens one month ahead. Max 5 per oversized-baggage booking, so book the 6 of us in two goes.
  • Or skip the bags: Send suitcases ahead by takkyubin the day before.
